# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone who participates in the Agent Skill Security project.

We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Provide helpful feedback.
- Focus on technical discussions.
- Respect different viewpoints.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Publishing private information.
- Malicious contributions or intentionally harmful code.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience unacceptable behavior, please report it through the project's issue tracker or security reporting channel.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a safe and productive community.
